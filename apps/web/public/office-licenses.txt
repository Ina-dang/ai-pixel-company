AI Pixel Company original office v3

Current artwork and presentation:
  Tiles, furniture, characters and layout are original procedural AI Pixel
  Company artwork dedicated to CC0-1.0. No downloaded images, external fonts,
  paid asset packs or image-generation APIs are used.
  Room names, employee labels and activity text are HTML.
  The former pixel-agents palette adaptation has been removed from this version.

Rendering dependency: Phaser 3.90.0
  https://github.com/phaserjs/phaser
  License below matches the installed Phaser distribution.
